Gabay 1.0 · free for Windows 11 and 10

Gabay.

Your Windows taskbar, folded into an iPhone-style notch.

Gabay is Filipino for "guide". It sits at the top of your screen, shows what matters at a glance, and opens into quick settings, your day and your tools when you hover over it.

Download for Windows How to install

Windows installer · free

Installs for your account only and starts with Windows. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time: choose More info, then Run anyway.

Under 0.1% CPU at rest No account No ads or tracking Updates itself

What lives in the notch

A status bar when you're busy, a control center when you reach for it.

Collapsed, Gabay is a slim strip with the time, Wi-Fi, weather and battery. Hover it and it opens into eight tabs. It steps aside when a video or game goes full screen.

Controls

  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on and off, with lists of nearby networks and paired devices.
  • Volume and brightness bars that drag like an iPhone, plus volume per app.
  • Focus silences notifications for an hour, until 6 PM, until 7 AM, or until you turn it off.
  • Screenshot the whole screen or snip a part. Record the screen with its sound.
  • Now playing from Spotify, your browser and other players, with seeking and optional synced lyrics.
  • Airplane mode, Dark mode, and Windows' Project chooser.

Live activities and alerts

  • While music plays: album art and a level meter. While a download runs: how much has arrived. While recording: a red dot and the time.
  • The notch grows for a few seconds for Windows notifications, a new song, charging, low battery, a finished timer or download, and a meeting 10 minutes out.
  • A green or orange dot while an app uses your camera or microphone.

collapsed · music · weather, download and camera · charging · notification

Today

  • The time, date and weather for your city.
  • A focus timer, a stopwatch with laps, and up to six timers that keep running when the notch closes.
  • To-dos that stay on your PC.
  • Up next from your Google Calendar, with a heads-up 10 minutes before.

Volume per app and System

  • A slider for every app playing sound, like Windows' volume mixer, one hover away.
  • CPU, memory and disk as rings that turn warm past 85%.

Notes

A scratchpad that saves as you type, one hover away.

Clipboard

Text and images you copied, with pinned items kept. Skips anything a password manager marks private.

Launch and Power

Install

Up and running in a minute.

Download the installer

Use the download button at the top. You get Gabay-Setup with the version number, about 107 MB.

Run it

Open the file. Gabay isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may warn you.

If you see "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway.

Meet the notch

When setup finishes, the notch appears at the top of your screen and a short welcome tour shows you around. Gabay starts with Windows from then on.

Windows 11, or Windows 10 version 1809 or later 64-bit Built and tested on Windows 11

To remove it: Windows Settings › Apps › Installed apps › Gabay › Uninstall.

Privacy

Your stuff stays on your PC.

Gabay has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It only goes online for the features below, and most of them are off until you turn them on.

What leaves your PC

  • Update checks to GitHub, where Gabay is published.at start, every 6 h
  • Your city, once, to find it, then its coordinates for the forecast (Open-Meteo).if you set a city
  • The song's title and artist to look up lyrics (LRCLIB).if lyrics are on
  • Your calendar's private address is fetched from Google.if you add it
  • A bug report, only when you send one yourself.when you choose

What stays on your PC

  • Clipboard history lives in memory and is gone when Gabay quits. Only items you pin are saved.memory
  • Notification text is shown in the notch and never stored.not kept
  • Your calendar address is encrypted with Windows' own data protection.encrypted
  • Settings and to-dos are small files in your user folder.local files
  • Screenshots and recordings go to your Pictures and Videos folders.your folders

Questions

Good to know.

Does it replace the Windows taskbar?

It does most of the taskbar's everyday jobs from the notch. Your Windows taskbar still works. Many people set it to auto-hide so the bottom of the screen is free.

Will it cover my browser tabs?

Settings › Experiments › Reserve space at the top makes maximized windows sit just below the notch, the way they sit above the taskbar. You can also set the notch to hide until you rest the pointer on the top edge.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Windows warns about apps that aren't signed with a paid certificate. Gabay isn't signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Does it slow down my PC?

Collapsed and idle it uses under 0.1% of the CPU and around 155 MB of memory. Its background helpers sleep until something changes.

How do I report a bug?

In Gabay, open Settings › Report a bug, describe what happened, and send it on GitHub or copy it to paste in a message. It adds version and screen details, never your files, clipboard or messages.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no account, no paid tier. Please share the link to this page rather than the installer, so friends always get the latest version.
